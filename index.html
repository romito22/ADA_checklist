How to read this
  • Questions answered: how many of the survey's questions were answered (Yes, No or N/A) in the restroom's current inspection. 100% means nothing was left blank.
  • Answers breakdown: the same answers split by result. Yes = meets the requirement, No = an issue was found, N/A = doesn't apply. "Not sure" answers are counted separately and flagged for review.
  • Status: Complete = everything answered · In progress = some questions left blank · Needs review = has "Not sure" answers · Not started = no inspection yet.
